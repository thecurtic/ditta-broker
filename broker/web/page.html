How DITTA works

__TITLE__

Your history is kept only in this browser, and only the last 20 searches. The broker keeps no history of your searches. It does keep a record of how each indexer answered each query, the query's words and the results included, which isn't linked to you or this browser.

Unredeemed searches

Paid one of these? Resume it — the broker checks whether the payment landed. Unpaid invoices simply expire.

Payment required

Pay this invoice with a Lightning wallet. The broker watches its own node: your results appear here by themselves the moment the payment lands.
Paid, but nothing happened?
Paste the payment preimage from your wallet's payment details and collect the results by hand. (Leave it empty to just ask the broker again.)
